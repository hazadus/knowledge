## Highlights
- **Spells consume FP and Stamina so it is important to pump your Endurance, and Attunement stats as much as possible.** This will probably leave you will a very small health pool, but you should be far away from enemies more often than not. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jdc0dmtksyzdyt7sxxswgjng))

- In order to optimize the build, we recommend getting you Vigor to only about 15 or 20 max. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jdc0dn0wttmdd2703bnefepr))

- **Intelligence stops scaling at 40**, but the strongest spell in the game requires 48 Intelligence to cast. Go for 50 Intelligence[ to meet the hard cap for damage](https://www.thegamer.com/dark-souls-3-stat-soft-caps-effects/). Invest to 26 for Attunement this will leave you with a large mana pool and four Attunement Slots. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jdc0dn6wydg35mq4s2j7p9te))

- If you took the Sorcerer as your starting class, pump the Dexterity and Strength to 16\. This allows you to have much more versatility in picking a weapon and allows you access to one of the best magic-based weapons late in the game. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jdc0dnarcmftbqpbv9qyg31t))

- There are three primary ways to get sorceries in Dark Souls 3\. The first is going to be by **finding scrolls and giving them to Orbeck of Vinheim**. He will then sell the spells from the scrolls to you. A few can be **found on the ground in the overworld**. The rest can be **transposed from boss souls**, so make sure to check before you consume each one. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jdc0dngne3cphrn00yybyxvp))

- The Heretic's Staff can be found in the **Crucifixion Woods in the same building that Orbeck resides**. This Staff is a clear upgrade over the Sorcerer's Staff. It has a higher Spell Buff than that staff, meaning spells deal increased damage. Additionally, it scales decently with Dark Sorceries which we cover here. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jdc0dnmahw72qwdjj3cm68fn))

- Leaving the Grand Archives, you will be assaulted by three hollow NPCs. **The one furthest in the back wields this catalyst** and will drop it when killed. **For most builds, this is the most powerful catalyst in terms of damage.** It also consumes double the FP per spell to compensate for its huge damage output. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jdc0dnsydn52tgwtqjfk5r5h))

- This ring is the starting equipment for a Sorcerer. If another class was chosen, **Orbeck of Vinheim will give the player this ring once three sorceries have been purchased**, and a scroll has been presented to him. **This ring boosts the power of sorceries by 12 percent with no negative effects**. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jdc0dnzsqz6vqrc8zz0xhz10))

- This ring lies behind a locked gate at the very beginning of the Irythill Dungeon. The key to the gate is called the Jailer's Keyring and is found in a cell right next to the very first shortcut in this area. **This Ring stacks with the Young Dragon Ring and offers a 20 percent boost to sorcery damage with no adverse effect**. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jdc0dp3g6h5henv3mr1n9f0k))

- The Scholar's Candlestick can be found in two places. It will be **available in Greirat's inventory** if the player sends him to pillage Irythill Valley. If not, it can be **dropped from the Scholars in the Grand Archives.** It is a fairly low drop rate though, so be prepared to farm if you never saved the noble thief. **This weapon offers 10 percent damage to spells as long as it is in the player's off-hand**. This spell buff stays the same no matter what upgrade level the weapon is at so you don't have to waste resources upgrading it. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jdc0dp71nz1wxf26fy9cbq3c))

- This ring lies **in the Crucifixion Woods in a pit with a sleeping Lycanthrope.** Though this ring does not offer a raw damage buff, **it does drastically increase the cast speed of the wearer**. This effect stacks with the Witch Tree Branch and can be further increased the higher the player's Dexterity stat. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jdc0dpctrbjdrj2wkb6ad5rt))
