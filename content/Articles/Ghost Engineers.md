## Metadata
- Author: [[Dmitry Kudryavtsev]]
- Full Title: Ghost Engineers
- Category: #articles
- Summary: A recent tweet claims that 9.5% of engineers are "ghost engineers," contributing very little while earning high salaries. The research behind this claim, attributed to Stanford, lacks peer review and relies on questionable methodology, as it includes mostly non-engineers in its expert panel. Critics argue that true productivity in software engineering is not measured by code quantity but by the impact of contributions, which are often overlooked in such analyses.
