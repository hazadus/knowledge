- Summary: Tracking Engineering Time provides a playbook for managers to better understand and allocate engineering time. The article highlights the challenges that managers without engineering backgrounds or those leading engineering from a distance face in understanding how engineers spend their time. To address this, the author suggests measuring time and dividing it into buckets that align with activities that influence output, such as features, debt, and toil. By monitoring and making adjustments based on these buckets, managers can gain insights into how time is being spent and make informed decisions to improve outcomes.
