- Summary: Personal knowledge management (PKM) is a practice that involves managing the continual stream of information encountered in modern knowledge work. Tiago Forte's four-part method for managing knowledge, called CODE (Capture, Organize, Distill, Express), emphasizes the importance of capturing and organizing information from various sources. Omnivore is a tool that helps overcome the challenges of reading and consuming digital content by providing a single reading inbox and a user-friendly interface. It also allows for easy note-taking and integration with other PKM applications like Logseq and Obsidian. By using Omnivore, individuals can build a valuable library of insights and enhance their personal knowledge management practices.
